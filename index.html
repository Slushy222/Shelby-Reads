Howdy and welcome to my book review website! My name is Shelby, and this is going to be a super chill place to put all of my book reviews for what I am reading. I mostly wanted somewhere that I could post longer reviews than what I do on Goodreads since that just isn’t my vibe there. I mostly read fantasy and romance, but maybe I will branch out soon and read some non-fiction, you never know (probs not). Also, if you notice that I give out a lot of 4 and 5 stars, it’s because I know myself very well and I have a knack for picking out books that I know I will love. If I read more outside of my comfort zone, I would probably give out lower ratings, but why do that when I can keep finding amazing books that I can give 5 stars to?

Also, go check out my Goodreads if you want to see a more complete and updated list of what I am reading! Not everything gets a review here, but everything goes on Goodreads!

Although I love reading, I’m not much of a writer myself. However, the exception for that is when I get to write about the books I read because that is actually fun! Don’t take my reviews too seriously, but also, if you think my reviews are good, then feel free to take them very seriously. :) Hope you enjoy!
